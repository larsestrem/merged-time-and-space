Moon phases 2027 FAQ

How many full moons are there in 2027? 12. Twelve, one for each calendar month.

When is the first full moon of 2027? January 22, 2027 at 12:17 UTC — the Wolf Moon.

Are there supermoons in 2027? Yes — 1: January 22, 2027. Those full moons fall within 361,885 km of Earth, so they look slightly larger and brighter.

What time zone are these in? Each instant is listed in UTC and converted to your own time zone when the page loads. A phase happens at the same moment worldwide, so only the clock reading changes — which is why a full moon can fall on different calendar dates in different countries.
