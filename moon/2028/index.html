Moon phases 2028 FAQ

How many full moons are there in 2028? 13. Thirteen full moons fit into the year, which means one calendar month gets two of them — a blue moon.

When is the first full moon of 2028? January 12, 2028 at 04:02 UTC — the Wolf Moon.

Are there supermoons in 2028? Yes — 3: January 12, 2028, February 10, 2028, March 11, 2028. Those full moons fall within 361,885 km of Earth, so they look slightly larger and brighter.

What time zone are these in? Each instant is listed in UTC and converted to your own time zone when the page loads. A phase happens at the same moment worldwide, so only the clock reading changes — which is why a full moon can fall on different calendar dates in different countries.
