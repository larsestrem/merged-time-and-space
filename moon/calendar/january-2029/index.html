Why does the calendar show a different date than another site?

A moon phase happens at a single instant everywhere on Earth, so the calendar date it lands on depends on your time zone. A full moon at 02:00 UTC is still the previous evening across the Americas. This page shows UTC before its JavaScript runs and your own dates afterwards.

What do the percentages mean?

How much of the moon's disc is lit at midday on that date — 0% at new moon, 100% at full. The shape drawn in each cell is the same figure as a picture.
