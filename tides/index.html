    Questions this page answers

    The question is the link. A short answer sits here; tap through for the drawing and the deeper pass.

    • What causes tides? The Moon’s gravity pulls harder on the water nearer to it than on the Earth’s centre, and harder on the centre than on the far-side water. The ocean stretches into two bulges. Earth then turns under those bulges, so most coasts see two high tides a day.
