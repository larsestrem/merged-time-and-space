Questions this page answers

  • What is a time zone? A political stripe painted on a rotating ball.

  • What is UTC? Coordinated Universal Time — the one clock the rest are offsets from.

  • What is daylight saving time? A twice-a-year decision to shift the clock one hour so evening daylight lasts longer in summer.

Tokyo time FAQ

What time is it in Tokyo, Japan right now? The clock at the top of this page shows Tokyo's current local time and updates every minute. Tokyo is on Japan Standard Time (UTC+9). The clocks there do not change during the year.

What time zone is Tokyo in? Tokyo uses the Asia/Tokyo time zone, known as Japan Standard Time, currently UTC+9. The clocks there do not change during the year.
